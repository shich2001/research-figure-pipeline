# Completion checklist

## Content and master
- Source facts, counts, labels and relationships are verified; excluded figures were not referenced.
- The complete master was explicitly approved before final asset generation/assembly, or prior approval is recorded.
- The final layout preserves approved hierarchy, proportions and relationships.
- Real samples and measured results retain their provenance.

## Assets
- Each illustration has the correct meaning and style.
- Each generated icon has real alpha transparency and visible foreground.
- No background card, title, count or process arrow is baked into generated icons.
- Real samples/screenshots are explicitly identified; opacity is allowed for those assets.
- Raster illustrations are not described as internally editable vectors.

## Visio structure
- Final assembly and exports came from visible local Visio COM.
- Backgrounds, cards, badges and simple geometric marks are native shapes.
- Headings, labels and numbers are independent native text and match the source.
- Arrows are native objects; any limitation about unglued line segments is disclosed.
- Each icon is an independent image object; no flattened column/full-figure image substitutes for editable structure.
- Object identifiers are unique; objects are not locked as a whole.
- Input spec and VSDX agree on expected native objects, text, images and arrow segments.
- The automated validator passed. It is a structural check, not proof of scientific correctness or visual fidelity.

## Export and delivery
- Inspect the actual Visio-exported preview at intended publication scale.
- No clipping, unintended wrapping, illegible notes or overlapping arrows/text.
- Verify one representative card, text and icon can be selected independently.
- Deliver VSDX and preview; save spec/assets/scripts and validation report.
- Explain native versus raster content and how to preserve manual edits.
- Do not rerun a rebuild over a hand-edited file without explicit overwrite intent; prefer a new output filename.
